BUSINESS DOMAIN: E-COMMERCE ANALYTICS

----------------------------------
CUSTOMER DEFINITIONS
----------------------------------

Premium Customer:
- A customer is considered premium if customer_type = 'premium' in the customers table.

Regular Customer:
- A customer is considered regular if customer_type = 'regular'.

Churned Customer:
- A customer is considered churned if they have NOT placed any order in the last 90 days.
- Order activity is determined using the orders.order_date column.

----------------------------------
TIME DEFINITIONS
----------------------------------

Last Month:
- Refers to the previous calendar month from today.

Last Quarter:
- Refers to the previous 3 months from today.

----------------------------------
METRIC DEFINITIONS
----------------------------------

Revenue:
- Revenue is calculated as the SUM of orders.order_amount.

Total Orders:
- Total orders refers to COUNT of order_id in the orders table.

Average Order Value (AOV):
- AOV is calculated as total revenue divided by total number of orders.

----------------------------------
JOIN RULES
----------------------------------

Customer to Orders Join:
- customers.customer_id = orders.customer_id

Orders to Order Items Join:
- orders.order_id = order_items.order_id

Order Items to Products Join:
- order_items.product_id = products.product_id

----------------------------------
FILTERING RULES
----------------------------------

Region-based Analysis:
- Region information is stored in customers.region.

Category-based Analysis:
- Product category information is stored in products.category.
